Enterprise-Grade Client-Side Security

Why OmniConvert is more secure than traditional online converters

Zero Server Data Leaks

Standard converters upload your sensitive contracts to remote clouds. OmniConvert executes all conversions directly inside your browser's V8 JavaScript engine.

XSS & Zip-Slip Hardened

Incoming `.docx` and `.pdf` streams are sanitized using DOMPurify and strict bounds checking, neutralizing malicious XXE payloads and infinite expansion loops.

Automatic Memory Purging

Allocated binary chunks and Blob URLs are automatically revoked via `URL.revokeObjectURL()` immediately after download, preventing memory leaks and forensic RAM scrapes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my confidential documents uploaded to any server?

No. PDFToolkit uses WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and modern Web APIs. The processing happens 100% locally on your computer/phone. Even if you turn off your Wi-Fi after loading the page, it works completely offline!

Is PDFToolkit completely free?

Yes, PDFToolkit is 100% free with no file size limits, no watermarks, and no sign-up required. Ever.

How does the certificate signature upload work?

Upload a photo of your handwritten signature (JPG/PNG). PDFToolkit automatically removes the paper background and converts it into a clean transparent digital signature stamp embedded in the certificate PDF.

Can I share converted documents directly to WhatsApp or Gmail?

Yes! On supported mobile and desktop browsers, PDFToolkit integrates directly with the native Web Share API, allowing you to beam files straight to WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack and other apps instantly.

Can I use PDFToolkit without internet after the first load?

Yes! PDFToolkit is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded, a Service Worker caches all assets. Install it as an app on your device and use all tools completely offline afterwards.
